Alderbrook Analytics
Business Expense Reimbursement Policy
Policy Code: EXP-310
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Policy Owner: Finance Operations

Section 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the standard process for reimbursing reasonable business expenses paid personally by Alderbrook Analytics employees. It applies to approved business travel and business activities in the United States. All limits are stated in United States dollars. Employees must use a company purchasing method when one is available for the expense. Personal expenses, commuting costs, and purchases made for family members are not reimbursable. Home office equipment is governed by policy EQ-204 and must not be submitted under this policy.

Section 2. Authorization and Eligible Expenses

Employees must obtain written approval from their direct manager before booking business travel or incurring a business expense. The approval must identify the business purpose, expected dates, destination when applicable, and estimated total cost. Email approval is acceptable if it is attached to the claim. Approval of the trip does not automatically authorize expenses above the limits stated below. Employees who expect to exceed a limit must follow the exception process referenced in Section 6 before making a purchase.

Eligible categories include economy airfare, standard hotel accommodation, local business transportation, parking during approved travel, and meals while traveling overnight for business. Airline seat upgrades, hotel minibar purchases, entertainment, traffic fines, and alcohol are excluded. Employees may claim only the portion they personally paid. Amounts paid using a company card must be recorded through the company-card process and cannot also be reimbursed to the employee.

Section 3. Standard Spending Limits

Hotel reimbursement is limited to $180 per room per night before mandatory taxes. Optional hotel services are excluded. Meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to $65 per employee per calendar day, including tax and tips. This is a daily total, not a separate allowance for each meal. A meal supplied by the conference organizer or included in a hotel rate cannot be claimed again. Unused meal allowance does not transfer to another day.

Ground transportation should use the lowest reasonable option that meets the business schedule. Standard rideshare services, public transportation, and rental cars approved by the manager are eligible. Premium vehicle classes require an exception. For destinations where no suitable hotel is available within the standard limit, employees must consult Section 6, High-Cost Destination Exceptions, to determine the applicable alternative cap, required evidence, and approving authority. Finance will not infer an alternative limit from a manager's general travel approval.

Section 4. Receipts and Claim Deadlines

Employees must submit claims within 30 calendar days after the business trip ends or, for expenses unrelated to travel, within 30 calendar days after the expense is incurred. Every claimed expense requires an itemized receipt showing the vendor, transaction date, description, and amount. Hotel claims require the final hotel folio. Employees must attach proof of payment if the receipt does not show that payment was completed. A bank or credit-card statement may support proof of payment but does not replace an itemized receipt.

Section 5. Submission and Review

Claims must be entered in the Expense Portal with a business purpose, category, cost center, and the required attachments. The manager checks the business purpose and coding before Finance reviews the receipts, limits, and duplicate-payment risk. Finance normally completes its review within 10 business days after receiving a complete, manager-approved claim. Returned claims must be corrected and resubmitted; the review period begins again when the corrected claim is complete. Approved reimbursements are paid through the next scheduled reimbursement run.

Section 7. Corrections, Records, and Support

Employees must notify Finance promptly if they discover an incorrect amount, duplicate submission, or vendor refund after submitting a claim. Finance may request additional documentation before approving payment. Employees should retain original receipts until the claim is paid and any open questions are resolved. Questions about ordinary claim submission should be sent to Finance Operations. Questions about destination-specific limits must be resolved through the exception procedure referenced above before travel is booked.
