Alderbrook Analytics
Home Office Equipment Reimbursement Policy
Policy Code: EQ-204
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Policy Owner: People Operations

Section 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how Alderbrook Analytics reimburses eligible employees for equipment purchased for an approved home office. It applies to remote and hybrid employees working in the United States. Employees who work exclusively from a company office should request equipment from office inventory. All amounts are expressed in United States dollars. Company-issued laptops are managed through the IT asset request process and do not count toward the reimbursement allowance described here. Employees should read the eligibility, approval, and receipt requirements before making a purchase.

Section 2. Eligibility

Full-time employees become eligible after completing 30 calendar days of continuous employment. Eligibility is determined on the purchase date. An approved remote or hybrid work arrangement must be recorded by People Operations before the employee submits a claim. Part-time employees, interns, and independent contractors are not eligible under this policy. Managers cannot waive employment-status or service-length requirements. Employees returning from an internal team transfer retain their original continuous-employment date. A reimbursement request submitted after the eligibility threshold does not make an earlier, ineligible purchase reimbursable.

Section 3. Annual Allowance and Covered Items

Eligible employees may claim up to $300 per calendar year for home-office equipment, including sales tax and standard shipping. Covered items include computer monitors, keyboards, mice, webcams, headsets, laptop stands, and monitor arms. Desks, chairs, decorative lighting, internet subscriptions, mobile phones, and gaming accessories are excluded. Requests related to specialized accommodations must be directed to People Operations before purchase and are reviewed individually. This policy does not establish a separate accommodation allowance.

The allowance resets on January 1. Unused funds do not carry forward. Multiple claims share the same annual limit, so employees must consider earlier reimbursements before selecting additional equipment. For example, an approved $220 monitor purchase leaves $80 available for other eligible items that year. Changing departments or managers does not reset the allowance. When a purchase exceeds the remaining allowance, the employee is responsible for the excess unless the order is changed before purchase to remain within the available amount.

Section 4. Approval Before Purchase

Employees must obtain written approval from their direct manager by email before placing an order. The approval request must identify the item, estimated total cost, business purpose, and vendor or product link. The employee must attach the manager's approval email to the reimbursement claim. A separate People Operations approval and an Equipment Purchase Request in the employee portal are not required under this version. Verbal approval is insufficient. If the item or total cost changes, the employee must obtain an updated approval email before completing the purchase.

Section 5. Claim Submission and Payment

Employees must submit an itemized receipt and proof of payment within 60 calendar days of the purchase date. The claim must include the manager's approval email and identify the Home Office Equipment category. The receipt must show the vendor, transaction date, individual items, tax, shipping, and total amount paid. A credit-card statement alone does not replace an itemized receipt. Finance may return a claim if the documentation does not establish what was purchased or how much the employee paid.

Finance reviews complete claims within 10 business days. Approved claims are paid through the next available payroll cycle after Finance approval. When documentation is incomplete, the review period restarts after the employee supplies the missing information. Employees should retain receipts and approval emails until payment is complete. A submitted claim is not a payment approval, and employees should consult the recorded claim status before assuming that their allowance has been reimbursed.

Section 6. Returns, Ownership, and Questions

Reimbursed equipment remains the employee's property. Employees must notify Finance within five business days after receiving a refund for a returned item. Finance arranges repayment and restores the corresponding allowance once repayment is recorded. Duplicate claims through another reimbursement program are prohibited. People Operations answers eligibility questions, and Finance handles receipt and payment questions. This version documents the rules for purchases made during the 2025 calendar year. Retain it when reviewing historical claims for that period.
